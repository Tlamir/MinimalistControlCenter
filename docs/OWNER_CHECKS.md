# Check the installed app

The owner confirmed startup and physical light-bar-off on v0.6.2, then reported a startup timeout on v0.7.1 after a later reboot. Version 0.8.0 adds longer boot deadlines and limited recovery. Automated checks cover recovery while hidden; the next normal reboot still needs physical confirmation.

1. On your next normal restart, confirm one Minimalist tray icon appears, the light bar turns off, and opening the app shows connected controls. Slow boots may take longer; recovery retries automatically. Report any final warning through **Details**.
2. Check the desktop shortcut opens without another administrator prompt, including after exiting the app. Repeat startup on battery when convenient.
3. Press the laptop's physical Turbo button while Cooling is open. The fan-mode selection should follow within about five seconds. Closing or minimizing the window should stop monitoring.
4. Check keyboard colors and the light-bar switch visually. Sleep/resume, OEM uninstall and full keyboard-effect coverage remain unverified.

No forced restart or stress test is needed. Keep the original controller installed with startup disabled. Custom curves and minimum-speed tables remain unavailable; see [fan support](FAN_SUPPORT.md).
